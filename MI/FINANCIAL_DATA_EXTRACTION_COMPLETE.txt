1. PRICING MODEL & REVENUE MECHANICS
================================================================================

DYNAMIC PRICING FRAMEWORK
Two-sided marketplace with demand-elasticity-based revenue split:

Demand Index D: Continuous 0.0–1.0 score (ML-predicted real-time for each lot)

PRICING FORMULAS:
  Lot Commission Rate L(D) = 30% − (15% × D)
    Range: 30% at D=0.0 → 15% at D=1.0
    
  User Convenience Fee U(D) = 5% + (15% × D)
    Range: 5% at D=0.0 → 20% at D=1.0
    
  Total AutoPark Take T(D) = L(D) + U(D) = 35% (CONSTANT)
    Always 35% of base lot price P, regardless of demand

ML MODEL INPUTS FOR DEMAND INDEX:
  • Time & Calendar (hour, day, month, holidays, school calendar)
  • Live Lot Occupancy (RSU spot count, lot API data)
  • Nearby Events (Rogers Centre, Scotiabank Arena, conventions, concerts)
  • Geographic Context (distance to downtown, transit proximity, density zone)
  • Weather (temperature, precipitation, transit disruptions)
  • Live Price Signals (Parkopedia, Google Maps competitor pricing)
  • AutoPark Network Data (OBU routing patterns to this lot)

DEMAND INDEX INTERPRETATION:
  D Range         Label              Typical Scenario
  0.0 – 0.2       Very Low Demand    Suburban lot, Tuesday midday, no events
  0.2 – 0.4       Low Demand         Residential area, weekday morning off-peak
  0.4 – 0.6       Moderate Demand    Pearson Airport weekday; shopping mall Sat noon
  0.6 – 0.8       High Demand        Downtown core weekday evening; pre-event buildup
  0.8 – 1.0       Very High Demand   Rogers game night; downtown Fri dinner rush; snowstorm

TRANSACTION BREAKDOWN EXAMPLES:
Let P = base parking price (set by lot owner)

AT D=0.0 (Very Low Demand):
  Parking Lot Receives:        70% of P
  User Pays:                   105% of P (5% convenience fee)
  AutoPark Lot Commission:     30% of P
  AutoPark User Fee:           5% of P
  AutoPark Total Revenue:      35% of P

AT D=0.5 (Moderate Demand):
  Parking Lot Receives:        77.5% of P
  User Pays:                   112.5% of P (12.5% convenience fee)
  AutoPark Lot Commission:     22.5% of P
  AutoPark User Fee:           12.5% of P
  AutoPark Total Revenue:      35% of P

AT D=1.0 (Very High Demand):
  Parking Lot Receives:        85% of P
  User Pays:                   120% of P (20% convenience fee)
  AutoPark Lot Commission:     15% of P
  AutoPark User Fee:           20% of P
  AutoPark Total Revenue:      35% of P

FREE LOTS (P = 0):
  Flat convenience fee: $0.99 cap
  Covers autonomous manoeuvre operational cost (V2X handshake, GNSS routing, RSU gate control)

5.1 PAYMENT PROCESSING FEES (GAP - NOT YET REFLECTED IN MODEL)

Per-Transaction Processing Costs:
  Stripe Standard:           2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
  Stripe Volume:             2.2% + $0.20 per transaction (negotiated)
  Adyen Enterprise:          Interchange++ (lower at scale, ~$0.40–$0.55/txn)

Example at $15 parking ticket:
  Stripe Standard:           $0.44 + $0.30 = $0.74 per ticket
  Stripe Volume:             $0.33 + $0.20 = $0.53 per ticket

Annual Impact at Scale (1M transactions/year):
  Stripe Standard:           ~$740,000 in processing fees
  Stripe Volume:             ~$530,000 in processing fees

Additional Compliance:
  PCI-DSS Compliance Costs:  $5,000 – $20,000/year

CRITICAL: Payment processing MUST be subtracted from AutoPark's net take 
before calculating unit economics. This is a direct revenue leakage.

8. KEY FINANCIAL ASSUMPTIONS & NOTES
================================================================================

PRICING STABILITY:
  • AutoPark take is constant at 35% per transaction (regardless of demand)
  • Split between lot and user shifts dynamically based on Demand Index D
  • Ensures predictable unit economics for financial modeling

HARDWARE COST TRAJECTORY:
  • Pilot (Cohda MK6C): $739.72 OBU + ~$660 RSU = ~$1,400/location
  • Production (Snapdragon 2150): $212.25 OBU + ~$340 RSU = ~$550/location
  • Cost reduction: ~60% from pilot to scale (standard semiconductor economics)

CRITICAL PATH ITEMS FOR GO-TO-MARKET:
  1. FCC + Automotive certification (6–12 months lead time)
  2. OEM NRE negotiations ($500k–$2M per OEM) — largest B2B cost
  3. Hardware NRE & prototyping ($275k–$750k) — must begin immediately
  4. Payment processing integration (PCI-DSS, Stripe/Adyen negotiation)
  5. Data aggregator API deals (Google Maps, HERE, Parkopedia licensing)

UNBUDGETED ITEMS REQUIRING ATTENTION:
  ⚠ Payment processing fees ($0.44–$0.74 per transaction)
  ⚠ Data aggregator APIs ($80k–$310k/year)
  ⚠ Hardware design NRE ($275k–$750k total)
  ⚠ Regulatory certification ($158k–$500k)
  ⚠ PCI-DSS compliance ($5k–$20k/year)

These must be incorporated into the business plan before financial 
projections can be considered realistic for Summer Competition submission.

MARKET SCALE ASSUMPTIONS:
  • Pilot: 500 OBUs, 50 RSUs (proof-of-concept)
  • Phase 2: 5k–10k OBUs, 500+ RSUs (regional)
  • Phase 3: 50k+ OBUs, 5k+ RSUs (national scale)
  • Unit economics improve significantly at Phase 3 due to 
    hardware cost reduction and amortized NRE
