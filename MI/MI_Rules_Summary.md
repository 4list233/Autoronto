# Mobility Innovation Event (MIE) — Year V Rules Summary

## Overview

AutoDrive Challenge II introduces the Mobility Innovation Series (MIS) requiring teams to develop innovative, multi-disciplinary solutions to real-world AV problems. Year V has one MI challenge and one 0-0-0 challenge, totaling 75 points (45 for MI, 30 for 0-0-0).

---

## MIE 1: AV Customer Experience (45 Points)

### Challenge Statement

Teams take the position of a **startup** focused on improving the AV experience for customers. Present a **product pitch** at Summer Competition to industry professionals as if gathering **seed funding**.

### Key Constraints

- Product/feature **must be present on the AV** — either physically or through the AV's internal software. Reward-based apps designed to encourage AV use are **not permitted**.
- Solution must be **novel** — not commonly utilized or self-evident within the AV space.
- Teams may target **Ride Share, Personal, or both** types of AVs.

### Definitions

- **OEM**: Original Equipment Manufacturer — the final assembler of the complete vehicle.
- **AV Customer Experience**: The overall journey and interactions a customer has with an AV from vehicle entrance to exit (ease of use, satisfaction, emotional connection).
- **KPI**: Key Performance Indicator — a measurable value demonstrating achievement of key objectives.

---

## Required Presentation Content (MIE 1.4)

Format: **15 minutes max presentation + 10 minutes Q&A** (30-minute block). Limited number of team members should present.

### 1. Elevator Pitch (5 pts)
Start with a **60-second elevator pitch** that captures the judges' attention. Must include an introduction, explanation of company/product, unique value proposition, and call to action.

### 2. Problem Statement (5 pts)
Identify and justify a product or feature that improves the customer's AV experience. This can solve an existing problem or enhance one's experience. Justification should include **background research and measurable data** that establishes a problem to be solved.

### 3. Pitch the Solution — covering these subsections:

#### a. Stakeholders & Impacts (part of 10 pts — Benefits to Stakeholders)
- Who are the primary beneficiaries?
- How does the product or feature help enhance their experience?
- Highlight benchmarking performed, compelling reason for judges to invest, and benefit to the customer.

#### b. Benchmarking & Market Assessment (part of 10 pts — Benefits to Stakeholders)
- Has anyone else tried to address the problem?
- If not, why hasn't it been addressed? What challenges currently exist?

#### c. Team's Solution: Technical & Customer-Facing Details (10 pts — Technical Complexity)
- What is the team's solution?
- What differentiates your product from others?
- Share a **system diagram and/or CAD/simulation** that explains how the product works.
- Include a technical explanation that differentiates from existing solutions.

#### d. Vehicle Integration (part of 10 pts — Feasibility)
- How does it integrate into the vehicle?
- Explain how the customer would experience the product/feature.

#### e. Business Plan (part of 10 pts — Feasibility)
- What is your business plan?
- How does this make money, draw more customers, or meet other self-defined KPIs?
- How much does the solution cost to implement? Include estimates for **hardware, software, and engineering design & development**.
- Must be practical and financially viable.

### 4. Conclusion & Appeal (5 pts — Professionalism and Persuasion)
Outline final thoughts and end with a strong appeal for funding and support. Presentation should be perceived as professional, team appears prepared, judges are persuaded to invest.
